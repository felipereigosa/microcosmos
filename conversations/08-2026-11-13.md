Not hard: about the same size of job as the paramecia, one session. I looked at the code and most of what it needs is already there.

**Easy parts**
- **The prey:** a handful of small ciliates (I'd suggest something like Cyclidium, about 20 um, which sits still and then jumps) is a simpler model than Desmodesmus.
- **The strike:** the neck throws are a scripted table (direction, length, dwell), and the prey's path would be scripted too. Aiming one throw so the head meets a prey at the right moment is just arithmetic.

- **The swallow itself:** really, the head touches the prey, toxicysts paralyse it, the mouth stretches far wider than the head, and the lump slides down the neck into the body. - **The loop:** the eaten one has to be back by the time the loop restarts. The simplest honest fix is a replacement swimming in from outside the frame later in the loop.
- **Reference first:** as with everything else, I'd find real feeding footage before building, so the timing and the stretch of the mouth are copied, not invented.

The first version would be one catch per loop, with 5 or 6 prey around the flocs. The first thing I'd show you is stills of the strike and the swallow.
