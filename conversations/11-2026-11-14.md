What you should see: the page's view straight ahead of you, about twice as big as on the page, 1.5 m away, in stereo. There is no bloom in VR.

Navigation:
- **Trigger or grip, and drag:** pulls the water along with your hand.
- **Both hands held:** move them apart or together to grow or shrink the scene, and twist to turn it any way round.
- **Left stick:** fly in the direction you are looking.
- **Right stick:** sideways turns you in 30 degree steps; forward and back grows and shrinks the scene.

The controllers are not drawn, and clicking an organism does nothing in VR yet.

Nothing was published to get it there. The laptop serves the page on its own localhost (port 8377) and the Quest reaches it over adb, at `http://localhost:8377/microcosm.html` in the Quest browser. That link breaks if the Quest restarts or drops off adb, and the small server on the laptop stops when you log out (or with `systemctl --user stop microcosm-vr`).

The new code is in `web/src/vr.js`, with small changes to `main.js` and the template. Nothing is committed.
