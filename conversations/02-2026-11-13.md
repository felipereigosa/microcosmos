## Felipe

Ok, the simulation looks pretty dope but there's some flickering. It's as if the whole screen is black for a fraction of a second. Any ideas?

## Claude

Found it and fixed it. Reload `web/microcosm.html` and the flashes should be gone.

**Cause:** the green filaments' shader computed a rim glow with `pow()` on a value that rounding could push a hair below zero, which happens where a tube faces the camera dead on. That gives one invalid (NaN) pixel, and the bloom pass smears it over the whole frame, so the screen goes black for that one frame. This is why it showed mostly when looking from above.

**Fix:** I clamped that value in the filament shader, and did the same in the two other shaders with the same pattern (the animal's skin and the coloured shells). It's in `web/src/main.js`, with `microcosm.html` rebuilt.

**Check:** in the test Chromium I swept the camera around while it played and counted fully black frames:

| Run | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| 60 s sweep near top-down | 5 black of 3114 frames | 0 of 2809 |
| 45 s sweep over all angles and distances | 2 black of 2077 | 0 of 2007 |

Before the fix, hiding one filament at a time made each black frame go away, which is how I pinned it on them.

That test ran on the laptop's Intel graphics in Chromium, not in Firefox, so if you still see a flash there, tell me roughly what the camera was doing. Nothing is committed yet.
